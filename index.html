    And then the rest of it

    And everywhere else the same thing is happening.

    The five above are where most people start. The same approach works on every other part of the business that only breaks because a person was busy, tired, or never told. What that looks like:

    • Sales. Every lead from every source landing in one place, in the same shape, chased on a schedule instead of whenever someone remembers. The follow-up that was supposed to happen in ninety days and never does.
    • Quotes and proposals. The document that takes an hour to assemble, built from what the customer already told you, and then chased until they answer either way.
    • Money in. Invoices that go out the day the work is done. Payment reminders that go out without you deciding to send them. What you are owed and have not been paid, worked every week instead of whenever it gets bad enough to notice.
    • Money out and what it means. Job costing that tells you which work is actually profitable, and the weekly numbers you rebuild in a spreadsheet every Monday, built for you instead.
    • Scheduling. A calendar that fills its own gaps, and appointment reminders that cut the no-shows you currently absorb as a cost of doing business.
    • Admin and paperwork. Intake that stops getting typed twice into two different systems. Warranty, renewal, and recall outreach nobody has time to run by hand. Onboarding a new hire without walking them through the same eight things you walked the last one through.

    We do not sell you a product and then look for a use. We walk the whole operation first, find what is actually costing you money, and build that. Sometimes it is the phone. Sometimes it is one thing you have complained about for two years and stopped noticing. It comes back as one engagement and one number, not a menu you assemble yourself.

    Before you book

    The questions everybody asks.

    How much does this cost?

    Builds start at $3,500. What moves that number is how many things we are fixing and how tangled what you have now is. On top of the build there is a monthly retainer that scales with how much we are running for you.

    We will not quote you before we have looked at your operation, because we would be guessing, and a number we made up is worse than no number at all.

    How fast is it running?

    The first pieces are live in about a week. We start with whatever we can prove is losing you money fastest, usually the phone, and build out from there.

    Do you need access to my software?

    Usually less than people expect. A lot of what we build runs alongside what you already have rather than inside it.

    Where we do need to connect something, we tell you exactly what and why before it gets built, and you can say no to any piece of it. Nothing gets access it does not need. If a fix genuinely requires writing into your dispatch, booking or accounting system, that is a separate conversation and a separate quote, and we would tell you that up front rather than after.

    Is it going to sound like a robot to my customers?

    Most of what we build your customers never see or hear at all. Invoicing, scheduling, reporting and the handoffs between your own people all happen behind the scenes.

    Where something does talk to a customer, it answers immediately, it knows what you sell and what you charge, and it asks what your front desk would ask. It will not pretend to be something it is not, diagnose a problem, or quote a price. And if your customers would genuinely be better served by a person on that line, we will tell you that.

    What if it does not work for my business?

    The agreement is month to month and either of us can end it with thirty days' notice. You are not signing up for a year.

    The setup fee covers work that has already been done, so it is not refundable once we have started, and we would rather say that plainly now than surprise you with it later. If we do not think we can help you, we will tell you on the first call, before you have paid anything.

    Am I locked in?

    No. Month to month, thirty days' notice, and if you ever leave, the phone number we set up gets ported to you. You keep it.
