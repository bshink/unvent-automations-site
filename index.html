What you get

Five things, running from week one.

01

The phone always gets answered

Around the clock. It knows what you sell, what you charge, and who you serve. Nights, weekends, lunch, and the ninety seconds everyone is heads-down. It replaces the voicemail nobody checks and the front desk you cannot justify hiring.

Missed calls get a text back

And then the rest of it

Those five are where we start, because that is where the money is easiest to prove.

Once the phone is handled, the same approach works on everything else that only breaks because a person is busy, tired, or was never told. What that looks like:

  • Scheduling and dispatch that fills its own gaps instead of waiting on someone to work the calendar
  • Intake and paperwork that stops getting typed twice into two different systems
  • Invoices that go out the day the work is done, and payment reminders that go out without you deciding to send them
  • Appointment reminders that cut the no-shows you currently absorb as a cost of doing business
  • Warranty, renewal, and recall outreach nobody has time to run by hand
  • The weekly numbers you rebuild in a spreadsheet every Monday, built for you instead
  • Every lead from every source landing in one place, in the same shape, without anyone copying anything
  • The follow-up that was supposed to happen in ninety days and never does

We do not sell you a product and then look for a use. We walk the whole operation first, find what is actually costing you money, and build that. Sometimes it is all five of the above. Sometimes it is one thing you have complained about for two years and stopped noticing.
