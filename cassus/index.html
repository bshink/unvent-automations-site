01 · How it works

We look before we build anything.

You have run this for years. We have been looking at it since Tuesday. Only one of us should be doing most of the talking on the first call.

We walk the whole operation

What goes wrong most often. What happens between a customer getting in touch and somebody actually doing the work. Where the same information gets typed twice. Who is the one person who knows how a thing works, and what happens the week they are away.

We put a number on every gap

Each one costed in dollars from your own figures, with the arithmetic shown. Some of it will be smaller than you feared. Some of it will be worse. You get the number on the first call, before you have paid anything.

We build what pays first

Usually the expensive problem is not the one you called about. Sometimes you tell us we are wrong, and that is a good call rather than a failed one. First systems are live in about a week, starting with whatever we can prove is costing the most.

You keep the controls.

Every conversation about handing work to a machine reaches the same moment, where you lean back and think: so I lose control of my own business. Reasonable worry, wrong one. The machine does the repetition. You keep the authority, and you can stop any part of it at any time. Nothing goes live until you have seen it and said yes.

02 · What we build

If you do it every week, it can run itself.

Anything that happens on a schedule, or only ever breaks because somebody was busy, tired, or never told. Grouped below by the problem you would actually describe out loud.

Money in

Invoices drafted the day the work is done. Payment reminders that keep going past the point your accounting software gives up, that read the reply, and that escalate to you when the answer actually needs a person. Deposits, payment plans, failed cards.

Quotes and follow-up

The estimate that dies because chasing it is nobody's job, followed up by text and email until they answer either way. Plus a second pass months later on the ones that went quiet.

Systems that talk

Your CRM, calendar, quoting tool and scheduler sharing records instead of everything being typed three times. If what you have genuinely cannot connect, we build you one that fits how you actually work rather than moving you onto something worse.

The 9pm admin

Scheduling, reminders, intake, job routing, timesheets, purchase orders, stock alerts, warranty registrations, permit tracking. The parts of the day that are identical every time.

Waking up old customers

Hundreds of past customers who would hire you again and have not heard from you in two years. Worked against a list you already own, which makes it the cheapest revenue in your business.

One place to see it all

Three numbers on your phone: what got handled, what is sitting waiting on a person, and what fell through. Plus whatever weekly report you currently rebuild in a spreadsheet.

Your people and your paperwork

Onboarding and offboarding, certifications and licences that expire, insurance certificates, contract renewals, documents out for signature. The things that are fine until the one time they are not.

Anything that repeats

Every business has two or three jobs nobody would miss, done by hand, every week, because that is how it has always been done. The list above is what comes up most, not a menu we are limited to. If it repeats and it costs you money or hours, bring it to the call.

04 · Where we stop

Five things we will not do.

Said now, rather than after you have paid for something.

  • Guess at a price before we have looked at your operation. A number we made up is worse than no number at all.
  • Tell you how to run your business. You know it better than we will. We build what you approve and nothing else.
  • Pretend a machine is a person to your customers. Most of what we build your customers never see. Where something does reach them, it will not pretend to be something it is not.
  • Lock you in. Month to month, thirty days notice, and what we built stays yours.
  • Take your money if we cannot help you. We say so on the first call, before you have paid anything.
What actually happens on the first call.

Thirty minutes, nothing to sign. We ask what goes wrong most often and you tell us. You leave with a number on what it is costing you, whether or not you ever hire us. If we do not think we can help, that is what we will say.
