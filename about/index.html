About

We find what your operation is losing, and we build the fix.

The first call is thirty minutes and mostly questions. You leave it with every gap we found costed in dollars, whether or not you hire us. If you do, the first systems are running about a week later.

We want you to get to Friday and not be able to name the thing you dropped.

That is the only thing we measure against. Not headcount, not seats, not a dashboard nobody opens.

The part everyone worries about

You keep the controls.

Every conversation about handing work to a machine reaches the same moment, where the owner leans back and says some version of: so I lose control of my own business. Reasonable worry. Wrong one. Toyota runs one of the most automated lines on earth and still puts a cord within reach of every person standing at it.

The machine

Perfect ten thousand times. Blind to the wrong one.

The person

Bad at ten thousand. Very good at the one.

The cord

Toyota gave every worker one. Pull it, the line stops.

The machine does the repeating. You keep the authority. Before we build anything we ask one question about it: if this does the wrong thing on a Tuesday, how fast do you find out and how fast can you stop it? If the answer is that you find out in a month from an angry customer, we do not build it.

How we engage

A limited number of engagements at a time, by design.

Every build is different and we are inside the operation while we make it. That is not work that gets better by adding accounts, so we do not add them. Month to month, thirty days' notice. We would rather be kept for what the systems return than for how long the contract runs.
